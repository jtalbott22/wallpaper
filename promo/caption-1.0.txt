Aether 1.0 is out 🎉

A live wallpaper of particles, fluids and fractals that never repeats: 16 real-time GPU simulations (galaxies, silk, particle life, reaction-diffusion, a ray-marched Mandelbulb…) slowly dissolving into one another, each with the math behind it a tap away.

It runs as the desktop on Ubuntu (top bar menu), macOS (menu bar) and Windows (tray), pauses itself whenever a window covers it, and the same engine is the background of my website.

Free and open source.
Try it in your browser: joshuaallentalbott.com/aether.html (link in bio)
Download: github.com/jtalbott22/wallpaper/releases
