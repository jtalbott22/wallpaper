The name

Aether (Greek aithēr, “shining upper air”): in Greek myth, the pure, glowing air above the clouds that the gods breathed, Aristotle’s fifth element; in 19th-century physics, an invisible medium once thought to fill all of space and carry light. Here, it’s light that fills the space behind your windows.

The story

Linux, Windows, macOS and your browser, in one evening. Aether started as a Linux-only wallpaper. Between 8:21 and 11:07 pm one night it became a WebGL2 engine with native hosts for Windows and macOS, built side by side by three Claude Code sessions on three machines. Read how or get it as your wallpaper for Linux, macOS or Windows, free.
